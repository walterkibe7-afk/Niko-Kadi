# Kadi rules (2 players)

- Standard 52-card deck. Deal 4 cards each, 1 card face up to start, the rest is the draw pile. (When the draw pile is empty, reshuffle the discards, keeping the top card. Proposed default, not final.)
- Turn: play a card matching the top card's suit or number. Otherwise pick 1 card and the turn ends.
- 4,5,6,7,9,10: normal cards.
- 2 / 3: opponent picks up 2 / 3 cards. Penalties stack: the opponent can answer with another 2 or 3 and the total passes back. Whoever can't counter picks up the total. (Whether a 2 can stack on a 3 is undecided; proposed default is yes.)
- Ace: changes the suit in play, or blocks a pick-up penalty (it blocks the whole stack).
- J: skip the opponent, so you play again. K: reverse, which in 2 players works like J. A J can be answered with a J, and a K with a K. The player who played the last J or K in the chain plays next.
- Q / 8: question cards. They must be answered with a matching-suit card. If unanswered, the player picks 1 card in the same turn and cannot play that picked card back during that turn.
- Declaration ("Niko Kadi"): when a play leaves you with exactly 1 card, you must declare with that play. Your last card is locked until you have declared AND the opponent has had a full turn. Picking up cards voids the declaration.
- Winning: the first player to empty their hand wins, and the last card must be a plain 4-10, never a special card. Playing J + a plain card to go cardless is NOT a win.
- Undecided: whether declaration is manual or automatic in the UI.
